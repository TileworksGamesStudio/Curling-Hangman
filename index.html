How to Play

1

Solve the curling term: Guess the hidden curling word one letter at a time using your keyboard.

2

Protect your rocks: You begin each end with 6 curling stones in hand. Each incorrect letter burns one stone.

3

Clear the end: Reveal all letters before your stones run out to win the end and advance your daily streak!

4

Daily schedule: A new authentic curling puzzle arrives every midnight. Released puzzles slide into your Vault.
